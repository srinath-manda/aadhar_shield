# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Aadhar Shield project! We appreciate your help and support.

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the Repository:** Start by forking the repository to your own GitHub account.
2. **Clone Your Fork:** Clone the forked repository to your local machine.
3. **Create a Branch:** Create a new branch for your feature or fix.
4. **Make Changes:** Make your desired changes, keeping the overall project structure and style in mind.
5. **Commit Your Changes:** Commit your changes with a clear message describing what you have done.
6. **Push to Your Fork:** Push your branch back to your forked repository.
7. **Submit a Pull Request:** Go to the original repository and submit a pull request. Make sure to explain your changes and why they should be merged.
